# Death Stranding

## Where is the options page?
The player options page is on the Archipelago website, or in the YAML generated by the Launcher ("Generate Template Options").

## What does the randomizer do?
The 70 main story orders (no. 1 to 70, from the prologue to Episode 14; 19 of them optional) are the checks. "Episode N Access" items shape the generation logic
(an episode is "reachable" once its item is received) and, with the `episode_gating` option, hold back the checks of an episode until its access is received. They do not lock the game itself: you can play the story without having received them.
Other items (equipment, resources, traps) are shuffled into the multiworld.

## What is the goal?
Complete the last order of Episode 14 (or Episode 12, depending on the `goal` option).

## How are checks detected?
The client reads the title of each checkpoint ("Order completed: No. N ...") and the end-of-order keys the game creates, directly from the game's memory, with no extra tool. Your saves are never touched.

## What happens when an item is received?
Chiral Crystals, materials, likes, traps and several one-shot item effects (Blood Bag, Energy Drink, Repair Spray, Boot Upgrade, Power Skeleton,
Speed Skeleton, Cryptobiote and Sandalweed packs) are applied in game through memory access. Equipment cannot be unlocked in game, so it is compensated by a one-shot effect or materials (Cargo Skeleton, Trike, Truck, Road Paver, anchors, ladders, weapons, grenades). "Episode N Access" items do nothing in the game itself.

## Optional checks
Likes milestones (`likes_checks`, `likes_step`), order-progress milestones (`order_checks`, `order_step`), standard-orders milestones (`standard_checks`, `standard_step`) and bot-orders milestones (`bot_checks`, `bot_step`) can be enabled in the YAML.
DeathLink is supported (`death_link`).

## Beta
Play offline, in single player, on a dedicated save: the client reads and writes the game's memory.
