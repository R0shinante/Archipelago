# Death Stranding

## Where is the options page?
The player options page is on the Archipelago website, or in the YAML generated by the Launcher ("Generate Template Options").

## What does the randomizer do?
The main story orders (51 numbered orders from the prologue to Episode 14) are the checks. Episodes 1 to 14 are locked
behind "Episode N Access" items. Other items (equipment, resources, traps) are shuffled into the multiworld.

## What is the goal?
Complete the last order of Episode 14 (or Episode 12, depending on the `goal` option).

## How are checks detected?
The client reads the title of each checkpoint save ("Order completed: No. N ...") after decrypting a temporary copy
with DeathStrandingSaveDataResigner (command-line version). Your saves are never modified.

## What happens when an item is received?
Chiral Crystals are added to the in-game inventory through memory access; other items are only displayed for now.
