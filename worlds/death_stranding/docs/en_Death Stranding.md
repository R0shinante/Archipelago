# Death Stranding

## Where is the options page?
The player options page is on the Archipelago website, or in the YAML generated by the Launcher ("Generate Template Options").

## What does the randomizer do?
The main story orders (70 numbered orders, no. 1 to 70, from the prologue to Episode 14; 19 of them optional) are the checks. "Episode N Access" items shape the generation logic
(an episode is "reachable" once its item is received), but they do not lock the game itself: you can play the story without having received them.
Other items (equipment, resources, traps) are shuffled into the multiworld.

## What is the goal?
Complete the last order of Episode 14 (or Episode 12, depending on the `goal` option).

## How are checks detected?
The client reads the title of each checkpoint ("Order completed: No. N ...") directly from the game's memory, with no extra tool. Decrypting the saves with
DeathStrandingSaveDataResigner (command-line version) remains available as an optional fallback; your saves are never modified.

## What happens when an item is received?
Chiral Crystals, materials, traps and several one-shot item effects (Blood Bag, Energy Drink, Repair Spray, Boot Upgrade, Power Skeleton,
Speed Skeleton, Cryptobiote and Sandalweed packs) are applied in game through memory access. Other items are only displayed for now.

## Optional checks
Likes milestones (`likes_checks`, `likes_step`), order-progress milestones (`order_checks`, `order_step`), standard-orders milestones (`standard_checks`, `standard_step`) and bot-orders milestones (`bot_checks`, `bot_step`) can be enabled in the YAML.
DeathLink is supported (`death_link`).
